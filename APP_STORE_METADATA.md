## Description (max 4,000 characters)

```
HablaBeat teaches Spanish vocabulary through music — by listening, singing, and playing.

Pick a song. Hear the Spanish lyrics. Watch the words highlight in real time as the music plays. Then learn the meaning of each word through three different game modes designed to make vocabulary stick.

🎵 50 songs across two galaxies
- Noun Galaxy: Alphabet, Body Parts, Roles, Pets, Travel, Numbers, Time, Feelings & Color, Food
- Verb Galaxy: AR, ER, IR verbs, Quick Past, Long Past, Future, Conditional, Pronouns, Advanced grammar

🎮 Three ways to play each song
- DDR-style bubble pop — tap Spanish words to the rhythm as they appear
- Sing mode — karaoke-style word-by-word highlighting to follow along
- Fly games — chase vocabulary words with a bunny cursor in themed mini-games

⏱ Three speeds, three difficulty levels
- Slower mode for beginners
- Normal speed
- Key Words mode shows only the most important vocabulary

🥕 Designed for joy
- A friendly bunny mascot guides every level
- Earn coins, unlock achievements, level up
- No sign-up, no ads, no tracking
- Family-friendly, kid-safe

Whether you're learning Spanish for travel, school, or just for the love of music, HablaBeat turns vocabulary into rhythm and grammar into game.

¡Vamos a cantar!
```

## Screenshots You'll Need

Apple requires screenshots at the **6.9" iPhone** size (1320 × 2868 px) for current devices. Recommended: 5-6 screenshots.

To take screenshots in the iOS Simulator:
1. Open the simulator with HablaBeat running
2. Choose **iPhone 17 Pro Max** in Xcode's device dropdown (this is the 6.9" screen)
3. Set up each screen in the app
4. Press **Cmd+S** while the simulator is focused — saves to Desktop

Recommended 5-6 screenshots for HablaBeat:
1. **Home / song picker** — show the colorful song grid (Noun Galaxy and/or Verb Galaxy)
2. **DDR game in action** — Spanish word bubbles flying across the screen with a song playing
3. **Sing mode** — karaoke-style word highlighting on a song
4. **Fly game** — bunny chasing vocabulary
5. **Mode selector** — show the Slower / Normal / Key Words options
6. **Bunny mascot moment** — a celebration screen, win screen, or hub featuring the bunny

Each screenshot should clearly show what the user can do — Apple reviewers and App Store browsers should immediately understand the value.
